Using this portal

Five minutes end to end. Nothing here moves your stock on its own — sending an order is a request; our floor does the work and you watch it happen.

  1. 1  Check what you have The Stock tab is your live position: on hand, reserved, available. Reserved means those units are already set aside for an order and cannot be promised twice. Sort any column, and download the whole list to Excel.
  2. 2  Tell us what is coming in Under Inbound, download the shipment template, fill it in and send it back. We check every carton against it as it lands, and you get a Goods Received Note showing what arrived, what was short and what was damaged.
  3. 3  Send us orders to pick Three steps on Send Orders: upload your order file, attach the waybill PDF (we match it to your orders for you), then send. Nothing reaches our floor until you press send — so you can start now and finish later.
  4. 4  Watch it move Orders show as being picked, packed and collected — the same words our own staff see, not a parallel set of statuses. Every stock movement on your orders is listed and downloadable.
Worth knowing
  • A SKU with no stock still appears, showing 0 — nothing ever looks lost.
  • If an order cannot be covered by your stock, we say so before it is accepted, not after.
  • You can cancel anything we have not started yet.
  • Up to five logins per account, each either full access or view-only, and each can be given a different set of tabs.

When something is not right

What we do, and what is worth doing at your end.

📋 A shipment did not matchWe record what we actually counted, not what the paperwork said, and the Goods Received Note shows both side by side. Short, over and damaged are three separate lines — nothing is rounded into one number.
📦 Something arrived damagedIt is photographed, held out of sellable stock, and shown on your receipt. It is never quietly absorbed into a count.
↩ A parcel came backWe count it in and get in touch. Your order says so, rather than continuing to read as delivered.
⏳ An order is not movingAlmost always stock: the order reads waiting for stock and names the SKUs. Send a replenishment and it moves on its own.
⌫ You need to pull an order backCancel it yourself from the Orders tab while it is untouched. Once picking has started, tell us instead — we will not silently drop work in progress.
💬 Anything elseTell your account contact — and say which order or shipment, since every record here carries a reference.

About IDEALONE

The warehouse system your goods are actually run on — not a portal bolted onto one.

📦 One live numberEvery unit is counted as it is received and again as it is picked, so the figure you see is the figure on the shelf.
⚖️ Nothing is promised twiceStock is reserved the moment an order is accepted, so two orders can never be sold the same units.
📸 Proof, not assurancesPhotos, batch and serial numbers, and a receipt note for every shipment in — evidence on hand when a claim comes up.
🕑 Rotation handledNearest-expiry stock goes out first, automatically, so old stock does not quietly age at the back of a rack.
📱 Built for the floorOur staff scan every piece on a phone. That is where your numbers come from — not someone keying a summary at the end of the day.
🔒 Your data, only yoursEvery screen and download is scoped to your account. You will never see another client's stock, and they will never see yours.
