Frequently asked questions

What is IdealOne?

IdealOne is order management (OMS), warehouse management (WMS) and transport (TMS) software for 3PLs, e-commerce brands and distributors in Singapore and Southeast Asia. It is developed by United Logistics & Distribution (S) Pte Ltd and used in our own warehouse operations.

Which platforms does IdealOne connect to?

IdealOne offers multiple platform integration: marketplaces, e-commerce and channel platforms are connected so orders come in automatically. Orders can also be uploaded from Excel or GI files.

Can IdealOne mark marketplace orders ready to ship automatically?

Yes. When an order completes its scan, IdealOne can set it to ready-to-ship (RTS) on the marketplace automatically. It also matches shipping labels to orders.

Is IdealOne suitable for a 3PL with many clients?

Yes. It is built for multiple clients and multiple hubs. Each client has its own item master, inventory and portal login, and admin features are switched on per user.

Do my clients get their own portal?

Yes. Clients can log in to see their orders, place new orders with automatic order numbers, and download delivery order (DO) PDFs.

Does the scanner work on phones and without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Staff can scan with phones, and scans are queued offline and sync when the connection returns.

Can I start with one module and add more later?

Yes. Start with Core OMS and add WMS scanning, TMS with the driver app, and the client portal as you grow.

How much does IdealOne cost?

SGD 80 per user per month with all modules included, or less when prepaid (SGD 76 for 3 months, SGD 73.60 for 6 months, SGD 72 for 12 months), plus a one-time setup fee of SGD 500 that covers client profile setup and onboarding configuration. Custom integrations are quoted separately on enquiry.
