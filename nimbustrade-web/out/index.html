Services

Eight service lines, run as one operation.

Each service below can stand alone or plug into the others — most clients start with one or two and add more as they need them.

  1. 01

    Warehousing

    Self-run storage in Singapore and Malaysia, extending through NimbusTrade-appointed partner warehouses elsewhere — every one held to the same fulfillment standards.

    Pay for the space you use, not a fixed lease.

  2. 02

    Fulfilment

    Pick, pack, and dispatch for D2C and B2B orders, connected directly to your storefront and marketplace queues.

    Same-day dispatch on orders received before cut-off.

  3. 03

    Freight forwarding

    Ocean and air freight booked and tracked through our carrier panel, with milestone visibility from origin to port.

    One reference number, not five carrier logins.

  4. 04

    Distribution

    Last-mile and regional distribution coordinated with local carrier partners, from Southeast Asia through to our wider international partner network.

    A single delivery SLA across every market you sell into.

  5. 05

    E-commerce operations

    Marketplace and storefront order operations, supported via file upload so nothing needs re-keying — API sync where a connector is already in place.

    Orders flow to the warehouse floor without manual re-keying.

  6. 06

    Cross-border logistics

    Multi-market shipping lanes for brands expanding beyond a single country, with local-market handoffs and Merchant/Importer of Record support to clear trade formalities in the destination market.

    Expand into a new market without hiring a local logistics team first.

  7. 07

    Customs & compliance

    Documentation and customs coordination handled alongside your shipment, not as a separate vendor relationship — including Merchant of Record and Importer of Record support where a market requires a local entity of record to trade.

    Fewer parties to chase when a shipment is held at the border.

  8. 08

    Technology integration

    API and file-based integration between your systems and IdealOne, our in-house operating platform — or skip integration entirely and run everything from the dashboard.

    Works for B2B and B2C clients, with or without integration.

How it works

Five steps from first call to live operations.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    We map your current flow — where stock sits, how orders arrive, and where the friction actually is.

  2. 02

    Proposal & rate card

    A written scope and rate card against the service lines you need, with no bundled extras.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    Catalogue, inventory, and system integrations are set up before a single order is picked.

  4. 04

    Go-live

    Orders start flowing through the operating desk, with a defined ramp period to reach full volume.

  5. 05

    Ongoing reporting

    Recurring visibility into throughput, exceptions, and cost — the same numbers we work from.
