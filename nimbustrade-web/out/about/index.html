About

One desk, coordinating the operating layer.

NimbusTrade Solutions was set up to remove the coordination overhead of running e-commerce logistics across multiple vendors — one warehouse, one freight forwarder, one customs broker, one set of reports. We run that coordination as a single desk, so our clients deal with one point of contact instead of five.

Fulfillment network

We run our own warehouses in Singapore and Malaysia, and coordinate fulfillment onward through a panel of NimbusTrade-appointed partners across Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Mexico, United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, United Kingdom, and United Arab Emirates — every partner held to the same fulfillment standards. You deal with one desk either way.

  • Inbound QC on every shipment
  • Same-day dispatch cut-offs
  • Real-time visibility through IdealOne
